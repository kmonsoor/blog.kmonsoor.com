Summary: Are you holding onto your death-trap?
---

To survive a burning fighter-jet, a pilot has to let go.

First, the roof blows off to let them out.
Mid-air, they drop the heavy seat that just saved them.
On the ground, they cut the parachute loose before it drags them down.

Every lifeline becomes a trap the second it outlives its purpose.

What safety is it time for you to let go of?
