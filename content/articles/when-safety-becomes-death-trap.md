Summary: Are you holding onto your death-trap?

To survive a burning fighter-jet, a pilot has to let go.

First, the roof blows off to let them out. \
Mid-air, they drop the heavy seat that just saved them. \
On the ground, they must cut the parachute loose to run to a safe place. \

Every lifeline becomes a trap right after it outlives its purpose.

What safety is it time for you to let go of?

Can be the job you don't enjoy anymore, can be the abusive partner, or might be childhood trauma.
Yes, many people "love" scratching their childhood wounds, to re-live the memory; again and again.
